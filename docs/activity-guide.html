01 · Orientation

Executive overview

ShadowTraceAI is a Snowflake-powered AML intelligence copilot focused on modern-slavery and human-trafficking indicators. It connects transactions, KYC, devices, external intelligence, and document metadata; detects network-aware typologies in SQL; and converts evidence into an explainable, human-controlled case decision.

5

Typologies

Wage harvesting, account control, spending anomaly, geographic risk, and sector risk.

92

CASE-C003 reaches CRITICAL through visible additive logic, not a case-ID override.

8

App tabs

From overview and network evidence through reviewer decision and audit trail.

Seed CSVs→ Snowflake tables→ Typology views→ Risk + brief→ Streamlit→ Human decision + audit
Important operating modelThe application can run in your local browser, but Snowflake remains the database. A fully offline database mode is intentionally not used.

02 · Why

Modern-slavery risk is a network problem hidden inside fragmented evidence.

Individual transactions can look ordinary. Suspicion becomes visible only when employer payroll, rapid onward transfers, shared control signals, weak KYC, risky sectors, recruitment corridors, documents, and external intelligence are evaluated together.

50M

People affected

The ILO estimated that 50 million people were living in modern slavery in 2021, including 28 million in forced labour.

$236B

Illegal annual profit

The ILO estimated annual illegal profits from forced labour in the private economy at US$236 billion.

1 ≠ proof

Signals need context

FATF guidance stresses that one indicator alone is unlikely to confirm trafficking-related money laundering; combinations and context matter.

Current-state failure pattern

Data
Transactions, KYC, devices, documents, watchlists, and media are reviewed in separate tools or queues.
Detection
Generic AML rules may not encode wage harvesting, shared account control, low personal spending, worker networks, and labour-intensive sector context together.
Investigation
Analysts manually reconstruct relationships, repeat searches, and write narrative explanations with inconsistent evidence coverage.
Decision
Risk reasoning, reviewer rationale, evidence gaps, and final outcomes can be difficult to trace as one chronology.
Management
Without standard measures, leaders cannot reliably compare handling time, evidence completeness, false positives, or control adherence.

03 · What

One governed evidence plane turns scattered alerts into defensible decisions.

The proposition is not “AI replaces the investigator.” It is “Snowflake assembles the evidence, SQL explains the risk, and a reviewer makes a better controlled decision.”

Connect

Unify transaction, customer, device, intelligence, and document evidence around a case and connected-account graph.

Explain

Calculate a reproducible 0–100 score with visible typology contributions, evidence strength, top factors, and route.

Act safely

Present an investigation brief and recommended decision while preserving named human review and a complete audit trail.

05 · Boundary

Prototype scope is focused; production design is explicit.

In scope now Implemented

  • Six synthetic seed datasets and two cases
  • Ten requested operational tables plus brief persistence
  • Five typology views and a case-network view
  • Explainable score, evidence strength, route, and brief
  • Eight-tab Streamlit reviewer experience
  • Four reviewer decisions with transactional audit write
  • SQL acceptance tests and CoCo evidence workflow

Production target Design

  • Controlled source ingestion, CDC, and data contracts
  • Enterprise identity, RBAC, masking, retention, and legal hold
  • Case assignment, queues, SLAs, comments, and attachments
  • Threshold calibration and outcome-based monitoring
  • Prompt/model governance and evaluation
  • Operational observability, incident response, and DR
  • Jurisdiction-specific SAR preparation interfaces

Explicitly out of scope

  • Autonomous SAR filing, customer restriction, account closure, or law-enforcement reporting.
  • Determining that trafficking or exploitation has occurred.
  • Use of real customer, victim, employee, or confidential bank data in the hackathon prototype.
  • Production claims about time or cost reduction before a measured pilot.
Core assumptionsAll identities and evidence are synthetic; rules illustrate a defensible framework rather than universal legal thresholds; business benefits are hypotheses to measure through a controlled pilot.

14 · SDD

Solution Design Document: Snowflake is the governed evidence and decision plane.

The design keeps data, detection, scoring, case intelligence, reviewer decisions, and audit lineage close together. The Streamlit layer presents and writes through governed Snowflake objects rather than implementing parallel business logic.

Logical architecture

TransactionsAccounts + KYCDevice/IPWatchlistsDocuments →Internal stage + typed tables →Five typology views →Case network →Risk summary →Brief + grounded prompt →Streamlit review →Decision + audit

Component responsibilities

LayerResponsibilityKey objectsFailure boundary
IngestionStage and type source recordsSEED_STAGE, CSV_SEED_FORMAT, core tablesReject malformed or contract-breaking data
DetectionDetect evidence-backed typologiesFive signal viewsNo case disposition; signals only
NetworkExpose account and funds-flow relationshipsvw_case_networkNo inferred guilt or victim determination
Decision intelligenceScore, rank factors, recommend routevw_case_risk_summaryRecommendation is non-binding
NarrativeAssemble deterministic brief and optional AI promptvw_case_intelligence_brief, optional AI_COMPLETEOnly supplied evidence; reviewer validates

ControlPersist decision and audit chronologysp_record_case_decision, decisions, auditRollback on any write failure

Production non-functional design

Security and privacy

Federated identity, least privilege, row/column policies, masking, secure views, encryption, secrets management, purpose limitation, retention, and access review.

Reliability

Idempotent ingestion, retry boundaries, freshness checks, release rollback, backup/retention controls, and documented recovery objectives.

Performance

Case selection and core views should meet an agreed interactive-response SLO at expected concurrency and evidence volume.

Observability

Pipeline failures, data quality, query latency, app errors, score distribution drift, decision mix, and human-gate exceptions become monitored service signals.

Explainable scoring algorithm

SignalDetection condition in prototypePointsCalibration owner
Wage harvestingAt least three workers transfer ≥70% onward within 24 hours25Financial-crime typology owner
Account controlCommon device/IP accesses at least four linked accounts25Fraud/device intelligence + AML
Spending anomalyAt least three workers show ≥80% transfer/cash depletion14Transaction monitoring
Geographic riskThree or more worker countries plus foreign cash-out12Country-risk governance
Sector riskLabour-intensive sector plus incomplete beneficial ownership8KYC/EDD policy owner
External intelligenceAt least one high-risk linked-account intelligence hit4Screening governance
Document evidenceAt least three risky documents4Document-control owner
Calibration ruleWeights and thresholds are prototype parameters. Production changes require documented rationale, labelled data, outcome analysis, versioning, regression testing, model-risk review, and approval.

Decision transaction design

  1. Validate the decision against the four allowed values.
  2. Begin a database transaction.
  3. Insert the named decision and rationale.
  4. Insert an audit event referencing the generated decision ID.
  5. Update the case status and assignee.
  6. Commit all writes, or rollback all writes on error.

Application query contract

All case-specific queries use bound parameters. The app attempts the active Snowflake session when hosted and falls back to st.connection("snowflake").session() locally. Cached reads have a short TTL; reviewer writes bypass read caching and clear cached data after success.

Architecture decision record

ADRDecisionReasonConsequence
ADR-01Snowflake is the system of analytical recordKeep evidence, logic, app reads, and audit lineage governed togetherLocal UI still depends on Snowflake connectivity
ADR-02Risk score is deterministic SQLReproducibility and control take priority over opaque scoringThreshold changes require governed SQL releases
ADR-03Generative brief is optionalCore service must function without model accessDeterministic narrative may be less fluent but remains auditable
ADR-04Reviewer decision and audit event are atomicPrevent incomplete regulatory chronologyAny write failure rolls back the full decision
ADR-05Prototype uses synthetic evidence onlyEnable safe, reproducible demonstrationProduction security and data controls remain separate acceptance gates
16 · SOP

Standard Operating Procedure: investigate consistently, escalate safely.

SOP-01 · Start-of-day service check

  1. Confirm the warehouse, app, source freshness, and latest successful load.
  2. Confirm no failed data-quality or SQL test gates.
  3. Review open cases, priorities, ownership, and SLA exceptions.
  4. Escalate access, freshness, or integrity failures before case review.

SOP-02 · Investigator case review

StepInvestigator actionEvidence/controlExit condition
1. OrientSelect the case and confirm customer/account contextOverview and case headerCorrect case and ownership confirmed
2. Validate signalsReview each detected typology and underlying valuesTypology SignalsNo obvious data-quality explanation remains
3. Trace networkFollow employer, workers, controller, funnel, shared access, and cash-outAccount NetworkMaterial relationships understood
4. CorroborateReview transactions, documents, KYC, and external intelligenceEvidence tabs and source linksEvidence confidence and gaps recorded
5. Challenge scoreReconcile every point and route; note contrary evidenceRisk ScoreScore accepted or challenged
6. Review briefValidate summary, hypothesis, missing evidence, and recommended actionCase Intelligence BriefNarrative grounded in evidence
7. DecideSelect allowed disposition, enter rationale, acknowledge human controlReviewer DecisionDecision saved successfully
8. VerifyConfirm decision and linked audit eventAudit TrailChronology complete

SOP-03 · Decision standards

DecisionUse whenMinimum rationaleNext action
Escalate to SARMulti-source suspicion meets internal escalation policyKey facts, typologies, network, gaps, and why escalation is proportionateTransfer to authorised SAR assessment process
Request more evidenceA material gap prevents a defensible dispositionSpecific missing item, owner, due date, and decision impactReturn to evidence collection
Monitor caseConcern exists but immediate escalation threshold is not metMonitoring indicators, period, trigger, and ownerSchedule enhanced review
Close as false positiveA documented benign explanation resolves the indicatorsEvidence-based explanation and residual-risk assessmentClose with audit record

SOP-04 · Safeguarding and outreach

Potentially vulnerable customersDo not contact a potentially controlled individual through a suspected controller’s shared phone, email, device, or address. Follow the institution’s safeguarding, confidentiality, SAR non-disclosure, and law-enforcement liaison procedures.

SOP-05 · Exception and incident handling

  1. Stop decision activity if evidence is stale, incomplete, mismatched, or exposed to an unauthorised person.
  2. Record incident time, affected case/object, user, symptom, and immediate containment.
  3. Route data defects to the data owner, app failures to engineering, and control/privacy issues to the relevant duty owner.
  4. Resume only after documented validation; never reconstruct missing audit history manually without approval.

25 · Recovery guide

Troubleshooting

snow is not recognized
Restart PowerShell after installing the CLI, run pipx ensurepath, or install snowflake-cli inside the active virtual environment.
PowerShell blocks virtual-environment activation
Run Set-ExecutionPolicy -Scope Process Bypass, then activate .\.venv\Scripts\Activate.ps1. This change applies only to the current process.
Snowflake authentication or MFA repeatedly fails
Test the connection independently with snow connection test -c shadowtrace. Prefer external-browser authentication or your organization’s approved programmatic access token.
CREATE DATABASE or CREATE STREAMLIT is denied
Use a role with the required privileges or ask an administrator to pre-create the database/schema and grant ownership or appropriate create privileges.
The CSV PUT command cannot find files
Run 02_load_seed_data.sql from the repository root. Confirm Get-ChildItem data\seed lists all six CSV files.
Streamlit shows “connection or setup is incomplete”
Verify .streamlit/secrets.toml, test SELECT 1 through the CLI, and confirm all six build scripts completed in order.
CASE-C003 is not 92
Re-run the seed loader, typology views, and risk scoring scripts. Execute tests/test_risk_logic.sql and inspect the first failing assertion rather than changing the expected score.
The optional Cortex view fails
Continue using the deterministic brief. Confirm model availability, cross-region inference, and the SNOWFLAKE.CORTEX_USER database role before retrying.
Plotly or Streamlit dependency error
Confirm the virtual environment is active, then run pip install -r requirements.txt and restart Streamlit.
